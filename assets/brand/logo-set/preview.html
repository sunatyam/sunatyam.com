Choosing a version

Light: ochre gold and burgundy on light neutral backgrounds. Dark: bright gold and ivory on dark backgrounds. White or black: single-colour uses. All twelve logo PNGs have transparency; the social avatar has a solid navy background.

Preserve proportions and leave clear space. Use a quiet contrasting area or solid panel over busy photography. Check fine details at the intended display size.

These are raster PNG exports, not vector masters. See the usage notes for guidance.
